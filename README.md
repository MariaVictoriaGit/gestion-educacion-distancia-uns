# Distance Education Proposal Management System — UNS

Academic case study focused on Systems Analysis and Design, requirements engineering, and UML modeling.

## Overview

This repository contains the documentation developed for the **Análisis y Diseño de Sistemas** course at the Universidad Nacional del Sur (UNS), during the first semester of 2026.

The work is based on a real institutional scenario presented as an academic case study: the management and evaluation process of distance education proposals at the Universidad Nacional del Sur.

The case examines the need to formalize and improve the existing workflow involving the university departments, the Dirección de Educación a Distancia (DirEaD), and the Comisión Asesora en Educación a Distancia (CAED).

The objective of the assignment was to analyze the problem domain and propose a conceptual and functional design for a web-based management system, emphasizing requirements specification, system modeling, and process traceability.

**This repository contains academic analysis and design documentation only. It does not include a software implementation.**

## Case Study

### Institutional Context

At the Universidad Nacional del Sur, educational proposals intended to be delivered through distance education must undergo an evaluation and approval process involving two institutional bodies:

- **Dirección de Educación a Distancia (DirEaD):** Responsible for the initial evaluation of proposals.
- **Comisión Asesora en Educación a Distancia (CAED):** Responsible for reviewing proposals and issuing the final assessment.

The original process involves several stages of submission, review, feedback, and approval. The academic case study explores how this workflow could be formalized through a web-based system.

### Identified Needs

The proposed system addresses the following requirements and organizational needs:

- Centralized submission and management of educational proposals.
- Role-based access for departmental authorities, DirEaD members, and CAED representatives.
- Notifications when proposals are submitted or forwarded for evaluation.
- Recording of individual opinions and institutional assessments.
- Generation and communication of formal evaluation decisions.
- Resubmission of proposals requiring modifications.
- Tracking of proposal status throughout the evaluation process.
- Monitoring of institutional deadlines and evaluation periods.
- Improved traceability of actions and decisions.

## Systems Analysis and Design

The assignment involved studying the institutional scenario and developing a structured representation of the proposed system.

The documentation includes:

- **Problem Description:** Institutional context, existing workflow, and identified needs.
- **Requirements Specification:** Functional and non-functional requirements organized by actor.
- **Business Concept Model:** Identification and representation of relevant business concepts.
- **UML Class Diagram:** Classes, attributes, and associations.
- **Use Case Diagram:** Use cases at the Elementary Business Process (EBP) level, accompanied by natural-language descriptions.
- **State Diagram:** Modeling of the lifecycle and possible states of a proposal (Solicitud).
- **OCL Constraints:** Formal constraints expressed using Object Constraint Language.
- **Quality Attributes:** Two relevant software quality attributes, with their corresponding justifications.
